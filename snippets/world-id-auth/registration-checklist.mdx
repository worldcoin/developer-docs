## Before you register

| Prepare | Decide before creating the client |
| --- | --- |
| Environment | Choose production or sandbox; use its portal, issuer, and credentials throughout. |
| Backend | Keep client secrets or private keys in a confidential backend, including for CLIs and native apps. |
| Identity sharing | New clients receive a unique, permanent sector. Sharing requires an owner-enabled sector and ownership of every application in it. A client's sector cannot change. |

Before replacing an integration, follow the account-linking guidance. A new
client can receive different account identifiers.
